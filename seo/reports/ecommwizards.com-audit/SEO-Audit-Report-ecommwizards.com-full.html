1. Executive Summary

This report presents a comprehensive SEO audit of ecommwizards.com for a Shopify / Shopify Plus agency (lead generation, booked calls) site, generated on November 16, 2026. Findings combine technical, content, schema, performance, visual, and search-readiness evidence as available.

66/100
SEO Health Score
Critical Issues Found:
  1. Gymshark-attributed testimonials and unsupported claims on 6 migration pages
  2. Fake scarcity countdown on /services/shopify-store-audit
  3. Inconsistent/unsourced trust claims (4.9 rating, partner tier, $400M+); badges unlinked
  4. Spam backlink surge: ~39 to 888 referring domains since May 2026
  5. Booking tracking gaps: Klaviyo bookings untracked, forms send no conversion, bookings store no source
Quick Wins:
  1. Remove fake testimonials/claims and countdown
  2. Add /insights posts + lastmod to sitemap
  3. Real reviews and linked profiles on /reviews
  4. Fix blog-post schema (image URL, dates, author)
  5. Migration CTAs to migration booking; calendar higher on mobile
